What is going on?

Each dot is a synthetic "training run": 9 model sizes from 100M to 70B parameters, each trained with 4 token budgets (D = 5N, 10N, 20N, 40N). Its loss is the ground truth plus a small deterministic run-to-run perturbation, an optional smooth bias, and rounding.

The fitter minimizes squared error over (E, A, B, α, β): for every candidate (α, β, E) on a coarse-to-fine grid, A and B are solved in closed form by linear least squares. The bootstrap resamples the fitted runs with replacement and refits 200 times.

Three failure modes from the original post:

  • Fit region — fitting only small models (as in Kaplan et al.) gives different apparent exponents than fitting a wider range.
  • Loss precision — rounding losses to 2 decimals (or reporting exponents to 2 digits) visibly shifts the fit.
  • Milli-loss bias — a correlated error of only ~0.05 in loss can swing the predicted Nopt by multiples at 1026+ FLOPs.
